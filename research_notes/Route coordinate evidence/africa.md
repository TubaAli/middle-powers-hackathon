### Inferences
- A first response is not approval; a filed or certified audit is not proof of remediation. The six-day Kenyan preliminary response is useful case evidence, not a national service-time estimate.
- Counting an internal information officer or DPO as an additional independent institution would inflate the agreement denominator. Vendor/data-holder access may, conversely, add indispensable organisations.
- The next pilot should collect receipt, completeness, first-query, decision and retest timestamps separately. Do not map the statutory clock or annual throughput mechanically onto a 0–1 probability.

### Gaps
All routes lack exact-pilot signed consent and a complete required-party list. The status is **found_partial**, not “private data” or “impossible.” Source-specific next retrievals appear below.
