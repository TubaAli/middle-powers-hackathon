## 3. Singapore: agent data-leakage deployment gate

### Existing evaluation workload and outcomes

The original SG/KR study uses **12 scenarios × three agents × ten repetitions × two institutes = 720 attempted runs**, **360/institute**. These are attempted runs, not 720 valid scored observations: NA and termination rules matter. The protocols use non-equivalent caps of **60 steps** and **150 messages**. No elapsed throughput, annual capacity or marginal cost follows from the run count. [Scenario design pp4–10; Table II p11; Tables III–IV run totals][eval].

On the Singapore suite, the fully **safe-and-correct** counts are:

| Agent | Singapore pipeline | Korean pipeline |
|---|---:|---:|

| B | 35/60 | 21/60 |
| C | 14/60 | 16/60 |

Source: [Table III p11 and Table IV p12][eval]. This joint endpoint is not leakage alone. Pipeline differences cannot be labelled treatment effects. These results identify failure and reproducibility issues; they do not estimate the risk reduction from imposing a production release gate.

### Actual government implementation precedents

GovTech's **2 October 2026** chat.gov.sg account describes **21 attack skills**, **ten agents/policy**, initial attack success **15%**, and **11.3%** after audit. Following guardrail changes, none of the previously successful attacks still succeeded. This is a concrete test–remediate–retest loop. The source does not provide the attack-count denominator, independent held-out validation, elapsed remediation time or cost. Zero remaining successes in the known-attack retest is not zero general attack risk. [GovTech case][sg-chat].

The **23 October** PSD/GovTech HR concierge case uses **four binary quality metrics**, synthetic conversations up to **ten turns**, agency-restricted tools, Litmus/Sentinel testing and owner remediation. Its phased rollout remains subject to readiness/approvals. This is operational integration evidence, not a nationwide mandatory gate. [HR case][sg-hr].

The official experiment, agency implementation accounts, and procurement-policy/award-data paths still do not identify the **binding release owner**, exact leakage threshold, actual eligible-agent register or gate-specific costs. The earlier **90,490 Pair monthly-user lead is excluded**: product-page retrieval did not recover verifiable metrics. It must not become a denominator by repetition.

**Next discriminating retrieval:** one agency's approved release checklist and delegated signatory, joined to an actual failed-test hold, corrective retest, active-system inventory and exposure counts.

## Retrieval discipline, limitations and handoff

- The JSON records each fact's population, period, evidence type, units, precise source locator, permissible coordinate use and limits. Publication dates are separate from event periods and observation date. `null` publication dates mean not recovered; English source titles are descriptive where needed.
- `found_partial` means useful evidence exists but the target field is incomplete. `not_found_after_search` is bounded to the listed paths, never proof of absence. Future consent/outcomes are `future_unobserved`, not zero. Access failures remain explicit in source/search records and in the reason for each affected gap.
- Critical unresolved empirical fields were pursued across at least three materially different paths as listed per gap: terms/plans, execution/experiments, and procurement/administrative/operational records. Some paths failed or were partial; this is **not** three successful independent confirmations. Future consent is not empirically discoverable merely by multiplying searches.
- Search-call status messages without result text are recorded as such and excluded as evidence. Two earlier shell-only leads—Pair metrics and an IMDA Google-agent factsheet—lack preserved exact URLs; their limits are disclosed, and neither supports a fact. Do not treat them as strong reproducible negative findings.
- Validation checked the exact seven route IDs, required fact fields, unique `ap-` evidence IDs, allowed gap statuses, all source/search references, every source URL's presence in this Markdown, and Markdown reference-link definitions. Primary-source spot checks confirmed Japan's award column/dates, the SG/KR results tables and Taiwan's annual-report/cohort figures. This is structural and selected factual validation, not proof that every unresolved retrieval path is exhausted.
- No installation, contact, spending, subagent or git operation was used for this handoff. Only the two assigned outputs were edited. The administrative status check returned HTTP403; no guide change was adopted.

**Highest-value follow-up:** obtain one actual enforceable instrument and matched operational ledger: Japan's November acceptance terms, Taiwan's next-cycle safety-conditioned award, or a Singapore purchaser's failed-test hold.
