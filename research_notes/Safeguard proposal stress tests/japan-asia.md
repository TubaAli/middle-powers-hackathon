# Japan–Korea–Singapore: procurement and deployment-gate stress tests

Cutoff and observation date: **31 October 2026**. Checkpoint: **21–22 June 2027**, as specified in the assignment. Six assigned route IDs are covered in the companion JSON. Four designs below are proposals, not institutional commitments. Scores and shared files were not changed. README, notes, DESIGN, the placement model, quantitative routes, coordinate evidence and its report were read before this refresh.

## 1. Does Japan's apparent advantage survive actual procurement and supplier constraints?

### Takeaway

**Qualified.** Japan has a concrete government release process, but the consequential question is whether a particular buyer can obtain, afford and enforce the necessary safety evidence for the actual deployed system. The strongest new evidence is an adversarial primary source: named supplier objections and the government's replies, not another programme announcement. [Consultation responses, physical pp33–38,46–48,69](https://public-comment.e-gov.go.jp/pcm/download?seqNo=0000315937).

### Cited Findings

- **Fact — independent safety versus internal review.** The final DS-920 guideline lists diverse/independent internal and external testing, including red teaming, among example pre-release methods; it also covers bias and inappropriate output. This is broader than cybersecurity, but not a universal compulsory external certificate. [DS-920 §6.3.3(1), physical p41/printed p35](https://www.digital.go.jp/assets/contents/node/information/field_ref_resources/decb64eb-f26e-41cb-8d37-f3dd173108b8/47764804/20260612_resources_standard_guidelines_guideline_04.pdf). Gen-nai's second reviewer is **another Gen-nai developer**, alongside approval by the responsible authorised official. Calling this an independent external safety audit would overstate it. [Gen-nai standard §4-3, physical pp36–37](https://www.digital.go.jp/assets/contents/node/basic_page/field_ref_resources/b033337d-dad1-4493-9316-d929597e4667/9bbaf559/20261020_policies_genai_guideline_02.pdf).
- **Attributed supplier positions, with official rebuttals.** Graffer and Rakuten say downstream API suppliers cannot disclose upstream training data or algorithms they do not possess; Rakuten suggests summaries or limited disclosure for secrets. The government's item-8 response says this is a negotiated, reasonable scope, not a uniform disclosure command. Elsewhere it says supporting-information examples do not invariably require submission. Conversely, when Graffer objects to upstream model changes/retirement outside its control, the government retains the original requirement and notes that important systems may demand action beyond that control. This is a genuine trade-off, not simple government agreement with industry. [Responses, physical pp38,46–47,69](https://public-comment.e-gov.go.jp/pcm/download?seqNo=0000315937).
- **Fact — migration and accountability hooks already exist.** The Gen-nai standard calls for loosely coupled components, standard interfaces, consideration/testing of migration routes and aggregate public usage reporting. These provide a better test target than assuming domestic models eliminate dependency. They remain policy requirements, not an observed successful failover. [Standard, physical pp8–9](https://www.digital.go.jp/assets/contents/node/basic_page/field_ref_resources/b033337d-dad1-4493-9316-d929597e4667/9bbaf559/20261020_policies_genai_guideline_02.pdf).
- **Fact — contract specificity.** DS-920 says to consider incorporating procurement/contract checklist provisions and add necessary items according to risk. It encourages public-market access for startups. A government-facing normative guideline is not proof that every supplier has signed every example clause. [§6.3.2(4)–(5), physical p40](https://www.digital.go.jp/assets/contents/node/information/field_ref_resources/decb64eb-f26e-41cb-8d37-f3dd173108b8/47764804/20260612_resources_standard_guidelines_guideline_04.pdf).
- **Source freshness correction.** The JFTC published version 2.0 of its AI market inquiry on **16 April 2026**, following further interviews. Its June 2025 report should not be described as the latest. Only the newer announcement was read here; no substantive lock-in estimate is taken from its unextracted report. [JFTC §§1–2](https://www.jftc.go.jp/houdou/pressrelease/2026/apr/260416_generativeai.html).

### Inferences

For `jp-procurement-safety-evidence`, keep platform readiness separate from enforceable independent-assurance readiness. A buyer can control admission to its workflow more plausibly than a foreign provider's global model release. Yet a hold has little practical force if the workflow cannot survive model retirement or moves outside the gate. This inference follows from the actual approval process and suppliers' upstream-control objections; displacement has **not** been measured. [Standard §4-3](https://www.digital.go.jp/assets/contents/node/basic_page/field_ref_resources/b033337d-dad1-4493-9316-d929597e4667/9bbaf559/20261020_policies_genai_guideline_02.pdf); [responses p38](https://public-comment.e-gov.go.jp/pcm/download?seqNo=0000315937).

**Proposed design 1: version-and-fallback acceptance annex.** One application owner, contracting officer and Gen-nai release authority would bind a deployment manifest, held-out leakage/unauthorised-action results and a service-utility floor to a release record. Unexpected upstream changes trigger a hold or a pretested limited-function fallback. Witness a consented staging failure, hold, fallback and independent retest. Failure conditions: no stable version identity, unusable fallback, bypassed hold or excessive false refusals. Proposed dates: scope by 15 December 2026; drill by 31 March 2027; operational receipt by 1 June. Cure, rejection or payment remedies exist only if the actual contract provides them. This operationalises the documented migration/approval policy and tests the supplier objection instead of assuming it away. [Design basis: standard pp8–9,36–37](https://www.digital.go.jp/assets/contents/node/basic_page/field_ref_resources/b033337d-dad1-4493-9316-d929597e4667/9bbaf559/20261020_policies_genai_guideline_02.pdf); [responses p38](https://public-comment.e-gov.go.jp/pcm/download?seqNo=0000315937).

**Proposed design 2: confidential evidence escrow with a public decision receipt.** A mutually accepted examiner receives necessary proprietary evidence under negotiated restrictions; the public receives the covered configuration, test scope, material gaps, exceptions and accept/hold decision, not personal data or exploit instructions. Compare an integrator and an upstream provider on evidence access, examiner hours, bid participation and review delay. Failure conditions: examiner cannot reproduce the mitigation, an evidence gap is hidden as a pass, or costs exclude otherwise capable suppliers. Proposed first receipt: 31 May 2027. Restricted use/fallback is preferable to an undocumented blanket waiver. This responds to real disclosure objections and the standard's transparency objective. [Responses p69](https://public-comment.e-gov.go.jp/pcm/download?seqNo=0000315937); [standard p9](https://www.digital.go.jp/assets/contents/node/basic_page/field_ref_resources/b033337d-dad1-4493-9316-d929597e4667/9bbaf559/20261020_policies_genai_guideline_02.pdf).

### Gaps

- **Not located:** the signed supplier acceptance/remedy schedule, lawful amendment path, final FY2027 tender changes, independent test quote and actual failed-release record. The repository's planned procurement calendar is not a newly verified completed tender.
- **Not measured:** incremental safety cost, bidder exclusion, genuine substitute quality, departmental/offshore bypass, gated request/token denominator or public-accountability effect.
- **Disconfirm Japan's apparent lead:** obtain one real contract/delegation and perform the staging failure/retirement test. If the right is unavailable, evidence cannot arrive before launch, or useful work escapes the gate, the programme's size and administrative maturity do not establish superior safety leverage.
- No India-specific retrieval was assigned here. A fair India/Japan comparison must use the same endpoint—an operationally exercised safeguard with matched activity and utility—not compare Indian allocations to Japanese enabled accounts. This is a proposed comparison rule, not a country ranking.

## 2. Do Singapore testing and shared protocols supply a deployable safeguard?

### Takeaway

**Qualified for all three testing routes and the procurement profile.** A particularly useful new primary-source limitation is explicit: Singapore tester accreditation does **not** certify testing independence or complete system safety. A common technical receipt can be portable; approval and remedies still need an actual national purchaser. [AIVF accredited testers, introductory disclaimer and competency scope](https://aiverifyfoundation.sg/ai-assurance/accredited-testers/).

### Cited Findings

- **Fact:** AIVF scopes accreditation to demonstrated methods and risk categories. Benchmarking, red teaming and leakage competence are not interchangeable credentials. The page expressly excludes an independence and complete-safety guarantee. No publication date was established for the live page; it was read at cutoff. [AIVF](https://aiverifyfoundation.sg/ai-assurance/accredited-testers/).
- **Operator synthesis:** The assurance pilot recommends application-specific test data, inspection of intermediate pipeline stages—especially for agents—and careful human calibration of LLM judges. Its scope is real application reliability, not simply model benchmark performance. These are practical workload requirements, not an itemised price or turnaround distribution. [Pilot main report, executive summary/recommendations](https://aiverifyfoundation.sg/ai-assurance/2025-main-report/).
- **Original technical research:** AgentDojo tests tool-using agents exposed to untrusted data and supports adaptive attacks. Clean-task failures are important even without an adversary. Its v3 record identifies an implementation bug correction: harness/version provenance can change the result, so an unversioned score is weak evidence. These are historical experimental findings, not October 2026 production-model failure rates. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.13352v3); [version record, 24 November 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.13352).

### Inferences

For `sg-agent-data-leakage-deployment-gate`, require clean-task utility, held-out attack success and tool-side disclosure outcomes separately. A system that always refuses can seem safe; final text can miss a tool-side leak. For `sg-purchaser-sandbox-release-gate`, competency accreditation helps select a tester but cannot establish the missing buyer's hold. These are design implications of the original research and accreditation exclusions. [AgentDojo](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.13352v3); [AIVF](https://aiverifyfoundation.sg/ai-assurance/accredited-testers/).

For `sg-kr-jp-shared-eval-evidence-protocol` and `jp-kr-sg-procurement-profile`, distinguish a common evidence format from common evaluation thresholds, recognised assessors and legally binding national acceptance. Application context and test competence are scoped, while Japanese contract terms are selected for the procurement. Therefore a coordination gain must be demonstrated in reduced duplicated work or additional bound activity, not inferred from the number of participating countries. [Pilot](https://aiverifyfoundation.sg/ai-assurance/2025-main-report/); [DS-920 §6.3.2](https://www.digital.go.jp/assets/contents/node/information/field_ref_resources/decb64eb-f26e-41cb-8d37-f3dd173108b8/47764804/20260612_resources_standard_guidelines_guideline_04.pdf).

**Proposed design 3: portable test receipt with national acceptance adapters.** Freeze model, scaffold, prompt, retrieval snapshot, permissions, language, execution limits, judge calibration and invalid/timeout handling. Exchange traces and an evidence manifest, then attach separate national accept/hold/retest decisions. Two labs perform blinded replication including a deliberately defective negative control and a clean-utility control. Change one field at a time. Failure conditions: missed negative control, unexplained decision disagreement, inaccessible evidence, or duplicated work erasing the saving. Remedy: withdraw portability for that configuration and require local retesting; the national owner decides the release. Proposed schema: 31 January 2027; replication: 30 April; national receipts: 1 June. [Design basis: AgentDojo](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.13352v3); [pilot](https://aiverifyfoundation.sg/ai-assurance/2025-main-report/).

**Proposed design 4: two-key purchaser acceptance pilot.** A scoped, conflict-checked technical report and a separate authorised buyer release decision are both required. Sign the fail/hold/retest rule before testing one sandbox application; seed a failing staging build and verify that the buyer actually holds it. Measure staff effort, delays, useful-task completion and gated transactions. Failure conditions: no legal/operational hold, thresholds changed after results, unresolved tester conflict or unaffordable burden. Payment withholding is available only if lawfully agreed; restricted-function fallback is an alternative. Proposed selection by 15 January 2027 and receipt by 31 May. [Design basis: accreditation exclusions](https://aiverifyfoundation.sg/ai-assurance/accredited-testers/); [pilot test requirements](https://aiverifyfoundation.sg/ai-assurance/2025-main-report/).

### Gaps

- **Not located:** an actual purchaser contract, delegated launch owner, observed blocked release, exact quote/queue, production activity ledger, signed trilateral recognition or confidential-evidence sharing arrangement.
- **Access-limited:** the direct SG/KR study download returned HTTP 403 in this refresh. Its bilateral run counts and pipeline differences were read in the existing repository dossier, not independently re-extracted; they must not be described as fresh evidence here.
- **Disconfirm the coalition advantage:** three national purchasers decide on the same packet; log additional tests, translations, exceptions, elapsed time and material failures detected. Reject the advantage if total work/delay rises without added protection or useful covered activity. Do not sum shared model users or duplicate the bilateral experiment across routes.

## 3. Can the Korean route be called legally ready, and what should the next research cycle do?

### Takeaway

**Unresolved for `kr-safety-case-incident-rehearsal`.** A candidate industry-submission endpoint was found, but its body could not be independently read. Its date-like filename is not sufficient to establish a later consultation. No draft incident clock is upgraded to law in this refresh. [Unread BSA candidate endpoint](https://www.bsa.org/files/policy-filings/kr08242026bsakr32ai.pdf).

### Cited Findings

- **Access observation, not a substantive finding:** Direct requests to the candidate BSA PDF returned HTTP 403; the document-fetch tool did not expose PDF content. No position, publication date or consent is attributed to it. [Candidate endpoint](https://www.bsa.org/files/policy-filings/kr08242026bsakr32ai.pdf).
- **Relevant contrast available from Japan:** Japan retained language that important systems may require action beyond a downstream supplier's controllable scope, despite Graffer's objection. This documents supplier-feasibility versus public-assurance priorities inside Japan, not a verified Korean position or bilateral dispute. [Japanese response, physical p38](https://public-comment.e-gov.go.jp/pcm/download?seqNo=0000315937).

### Inferences

A Korean voluntary rehearsal could reuse the proposed portable receipt's traces to test preservation, escalation, tool-disable and recovery. This is a proposal, not a legal conclusion. It must not be called statutory compliance until the promulgated notice and applicability to the operator are established. A tabletop completion alone does not demonstrate production control. No institutional consent was recovered.

**Next retrieval/experiment queue, using an adversarial researcher workflow rather than accumulating supportive headlines:**

1. **Authority:** try to falsify each assumed right with the signed acceptance schedule, delegation or final notice. Record the exact paragraph, current version and governing counterparty. Absence after bounded search stays unknown.
2. **Supplier:** test upstream access and change-control assumptions against actual contract/service terms and quoted evaluation work. Seek a capable small integrator's failed bid or exception record, not an imagined SME objection.
3. **Technical:** preserve clean-task controls, held-out attacks, negative controls and full execution manifests. Independently try to break the claimed mitigation; label results as staging/experimental until linked to production.
4. **Public-interest:** seek named civil-society submissions and actual public exception/incident records. Measure who bears false refusals, delay and service exclusion. The anonymous individual submissions in Japan's consultation are evidence of comments, not representative civil-society endorsement.
5. **Decision:** predeclare what would move the assessment down as well as up. The common endpoint is a valid authority exercising a tested remedy over identifiable useful activity by the checkpoint. Report non-adoption, exclusions and bypass alongside successes.

These are proposed follow-up methods, not work performed, installed tools or new standing jobs. The JSON contains claim-level sources, assessment implications, four fully specified design records, dates and explicit unknown states.

### Gaps

- **Not located:** final Korean safety-implementation notice and effective date, in-scope operator determination, actual exercised reporting/hold duty, completed corrective retest or final rule's cost analysis.
- **Named civil-society gap:** no exact Korean safety-notice counter-submission or named Japanese civil-society response was recovered in the bounded searches. Do not replace these with industry positions or invented endorsements.
- **Source coverage:** ten source records: six read, one partial (AgentDojo abstract/version record), three direct-download failures. Five read sources plus the research abstract carry substantive route evidence; the JFTC announcement is a freshness correction only. Three official Japanese PDFs and page-labelled extracts are saved under `japan-asia-source-extracts/`. `pdftotext` and Python `pypdf` were unavailable; macOS PDFKit extracted those PDFs successfully without installation.
- **Administrative status:** the session-start status endpoint listed the existing share-sessions guide version `2026-10-29.1` and no user items. No guide change was adopted.
