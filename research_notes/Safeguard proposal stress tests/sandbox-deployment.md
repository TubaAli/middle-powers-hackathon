# Gulf and Southeast Asian sandbox/deployment stress tests

Observed and evidence cutoff: **31 October 2026**. Geneva checkpoint: **21–22 June 2027**. Companion: `sandbox-deployment.json`; downloaded originals, extracted text and Thai page images: `sandbox-deployment-source-extracts/`. **Four designs below are proposals, not agreed projects.** No model, shared summary, commit or external publication was changed.

## 1. Which real decisions can carry a safeguard?

### Takeaway

The strongest new discoveries are **BPOM's AI-assisted internal procurement workflow**, **an actual Thai AIGPC operating contract with a narrower-than-expected scope**, **named AI graduates of Saudi Arabia's separate SDAIA sandbox**, and **Malaysia's original ADMP guidance plus a substantive consultation submission**. Dubai has a useful supplier-qualification mechanism, but its object is the company rather than each deployed system. Vietnam's missing signed local instrument remains missing.

### Cited findings

- **Dubai — qualified.** The official **20 January 2025** AI Seal announcement says government-project partners must be certified and describes company activities, specialist employees, projects and partnerships as assessment inputs. It does not publish a system-level held-out test or release veto. The accelerator, re-read at cutoff, accepts all technology-company applications, prioritises Seal holders, and accepts entities at different readiness stages, including already-built services. **Application eligibility and eventual partner eligibility are different stages: this is not a demonstrated contradiction.** Sources: [Seal announcement, partner and assessment paragraphs](https://www.protocol.dubai.ae/en/media-listing/news-events/dubai-centre-for-artificial-intelligence-launches-ai-seal-to-certify-trusted-ai-companies/); [accelerator, Overview / Apply / Government Entities](https://www.dubaifuture.ae/dcai-accelerators/). The Seal is new to this investigation; the accelerator is a refreshed baseline.

- **Saudi Arabia — qualified, with a stronger adjacent lead.** Myriota's own public account describes a CST graduation and an actual remote-water-meter deployment with **stc and Giza Systems for the Ministry of Environment, Water and Agriculture**, while calling full commercial service the next step. This is participant-reported operational history, not an AI test or independently verified safety effect. Exact publication date is not established from the relative-age display. [Myriota, post body](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/myriota_we-are-thrilled-to-announce-that-myriota-activity-7297857690528034816-v-dJ).

  A newly retrieved **SDAIA** register lists six named 2025-cohort graduates, including **Hojjah** legal AI, **Sahl AI** medical transcription and **Waki** children's biometric payments. The page describes post-exit monitoring; its personal-data track opens in **October 2026**, while monetisation opens in **January 2027**, still future. **The displayed 60-day service duration is not a demonstrated completion time:** its FAQ separately makes participation duration depend on the approved test plan. Joined/graduated dates are supplied as **Rabi al-Awwal 1447 / Shawwal 1447**, not exact Gregorian days. These are **SDAIA**, not CST, results and cannot upgrade CST's graduation or enforcement record. [SDAIA, tracks / participant journey / graduate list / FAQs](https://dgp.sdaia.gov.sa/wps/portal/pdp/services/dataregulatorysandbox/).

- **Indonesia — strengthened for a narrower pilot.** Pusdatin's **8 June 2026** report introduces **MORADA SSI** in KATALISTIK: AI extracts project-plan information into work specifications, and DIPA-funded requests must upload EGA SPBE clearance. The separate hardware module automatically rejects prices above prescribed limits. This identifies an actual AI-using submission workflow, not just general IT assessment capacity. The closing aim to end off-platform requests is **implementation intent**, not evidence that bypass is impossible. The page also contains an inconsistent ticket denominator: **765 + 773 = 1,538**, not its stated **1,488**. Do not use it for capacity scoring. [BPOM, SSI/APD bullets and closing paragraphs](https://pusdatin.pom.go.id/berita/KATALISTIK-MORADA). The control described is a **price/input control**, not yet an AI safety or DPIA hold.

- **Malaysia — qualified, with more specific primary guidance.** The original ADMP guideline's **§6.1** treats ADMP as a qualitative DPIA trigger regardless of intended extent; **§1.3** calls for earliest-stage DPO engagement. Its **§10.2.6** says AI must not be the sole decision factor, but sits under **§10.2's “may adopt” best-practices formulation**. Preserve that context rather than claim a separately enforced statutory prohibition. The official media record dates upload to **30 April 2026**, not necessarily legal commencement. [ADMP guideline, PDF pp.4,7–8,15 / printed pp.3,6–7,14](https://www.pdp.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Automated-Decision-Making-And-Profiling-Guideline-ADMP.pdf).

Its proposed Annex C separates a **standard-terms/settings checklist** from **negotiated clauses**. This is an original stakeholder submission, not official adoption or representative SME consent. The proposed notification/deletion periods remain bracketed. [Author submission, Buying AI / ADMP / One record](https://lpplaw.my/ai-personal-data-protection-malaysia/); [Annex C, pp.1–5, especially clauses 3,6–8](https://lpplaw.my/wp-content/uploads/ELP-AI-PDP-Framework-Annexure-C-Terms-Checklist-Model-Clauses.pdf).

Its status is contract/PO made, not completed work or paid expenditure. The separate award notice is dated **17 December 2025**. ### Inferences

**Six links must be demonstrated separately:** programme participation → applicable duties → authorised operational owner → valid test → binding response → improved user outcome. Supplier badges, purchased training, graduation, privacy forms and press releases establish different links; none closes the chain alone.

For frontier-risk impact, a local service gate is narrower than developer-wide control. For immediate public-service harm, however, preventing a wrong account association, disclosure or administrative filing may be more direct than adding a broad policy condition to a remote upstream input. Which objective is being scored must be explicit.

### Gaps

No exercised version-specific critical-failure hold was recovered for any assigned route. **Not found** is not **nonexistent**. The full Indonesian PP 33/2026 and Vietnamese local plan/AI02 remain unresolved; they were not silently replaced by press reports. The Thai award and contract metadata do not prove payment, final acceptance or completion. The separate Saudi graduates do not provide a matched admission denominator or failed/withdrawn cases.

## 2. Which four follow-up designs could demonstrate harm reduction?

### Takeaway

Choose a local boundary the named owner can actually control. Pre-register failure conditions and fresh tests, preserve service continuity, and make one observed **failure → restriction → correction → held-out retest** more important than a large cohort count. Dates below are **proposed milestones**, not statutory or agreed deadlines.

### Cited design anchors and proposals

1. **Dubai: action-permission acceptance independent of the supplier badge.**
   - **Owner/mechanism:** a selected service's authorised release owner; DCAI coordination and appropriate Digital Dubai/DESC review; acceptance manifest controlling production tool credentials. Programme roles are documented, but exact delegation and independent assessor remain to be selected. [Programme, collaboration and deployment](https://www.dubaifuture.ae/dcai-accelerators/).
   - **Demand/test:** no unauthorised filing, disclosure or irreversible action. Independently held-out Arabic/English documents, malformed requests and a planted critical failure must leave the affected action disabled. Repair must pass **fresh variants**, not only the known attack.
   - **Remedy:** disable the failing action, restore the prior approved version and route users to staff. A payment hold is available only if the actual contract permits it.
   - **Falsifier:** programme sponsorship or a Seal lets the same failing version bypass acceptance. **Timing:** owner/instrument by **31 January 2027**, drill by **31 March**, results by **1 June**.

2. **Saudi adjacent route: post-graduation drift and fallback audit.**
   - **Owner/mechanism:** SDAIA, one published graduate and its actual service customer; use the documented post-exit stage with a customer change-control agreement. This is explicitly an alternative to, not evidence for, CST. [SDAIA, graduate list and post-exit FAQ](https://dgp.sdaia.gov.sa/wps/portal/pdp/services/dataregulatorysandbox/).
   - **Demand/test:** choose **one** case. For Waki, synthetic identities test cross-child payment association, deletion and model/configuration changes while retaining card/QR service. For Sahl AI, test wrong-patient attribution and clinical confirmation. No experimental harm is imposed on actual children or patients.
   - **Remedy:** restrict the affected biometric/AI feature, correct records and restore only after independent fresh tests; preserve non-biometric or clinician-mediated service.
   - **Falsifier:** graduation is treated as permanent assurance, a changed system escapes review, or safe fallback is unavailable. **Timing:** scope by **15 January**, drill by **15 March**, retest by **1 June 2027**.

3. **BPOM: source-to-specification integrity in MORADA SSI.**
   - **Owner/mechanism:** Pusdatin's authorised SSI reviewer, the requesting/buying unit and the actual clearance authority. Tie critical generated requirements to passages in the approved plan and validate the clearance reference before procurement use. This is a **narrowed route variant**, not proof about the previously identified licensing AI. [MORADA, SSI requirement and integrated workflow](https://pusdatin.pom.go.id/berita/KATALISTIK-MORADA).
   - **Demand/test:** no fabricated clearance or silent change to cost, security, data access or evaluation requirements. Blindly mix clean plans with synthetic conflicting annexes and instruction-like text; compare the generated **and accepted** specification to the approved original.
   - **Remedy:** reject the generated specification, perform manual review, correct extraction and retest on fresh documents. Preserve legitimate requests rather than cancel the underlying service.
   - **Falsifier:** missing clearance or altered material requirements still enter approved procurement inputs. **Timing:** authorisation by **31 January**, drill by **31 March**, workload/retest results by **1 June 2027**. Confirm actual personal-data processing before calling the exercise a DPIA demonstration.

4. **Malaysia: proportionate controller-owned configuration and correction gate.**
   - **Owner/mechanism:** an actual commercial controller's senior manager, DPO and service operations owner; one acceptance record combining applicable DPIA, exact provider terms, settings/version and corrective actions. The terms/checklist distinction is grounded in the original submission; proposed clauses are not assumed enforceable. [Annex C, pp.1–5](https://lpplaw.my/wp-content/uploads/ELP-AI-PDP-Framework-Annexure-C-Terms-Checklist-Model-Clauses.pdf).
   - **Demand/test:** meaningful override of adverse decisions and no unapproved training/data sharing. Reviewers receive held-out correct and incorrect recommendations without knowing which errors were planted. Test correction after source evidence changes and detect a controlled settings drift.
   - **Remedy:** restrict the feature/data feed, provide assisted processing, correct the person's record and decision, then require fresh retest.
   - **Falsifier:** review rubber-stamps planted errors, corrected evidence never changes decisions, or the controller cannot restrict the service. **Timing:** owner/record by **31 January**, drill by **31 March**, fresh retest and correction evidence by **1 June 2027**. Thai or Vietnamese owners could adapt this experimental design under their **own** authority; Malaysian law does not transfer.

### Inferences: stakeholder stress test

- **Affected users:** measure completed legitimate service journeys, wrongful adverse outcomes, repeated visits, accessible appeals and time to correction. A stopped AI with an unstaffed fallback is not automatically safer.
- **Innovators/SMEs:** test whether fixed assurance costs and paperwork exclude smaller suppliers; reuse evidence only when version, tools and use context match. Company age or a badge should not substitute for performance.
- **Regulator and assessor capacity:** log reviewer hours, queue time, disagreement and missed seeded failures. Separate training/adoption support from independent scoring and disclose conflicts; do not presume a new agency is necessary.
- **Procurement owner:** distinguish withholding an unaccepted deliverable from suspending a citizen service. Test the actual delegation and contract before promising either remedy.
- **International substitutability:** a controller may switch models or restrict tools, but replacement costs and equivalent functionality need observation. A local wrapper gate does not bind every overseas use of the same model; an exported blueprint needs revalidation in each service context.
- **Causal evaluation:** record baseline and post-change error rates on matched tasks, false holds, fallback failures and review cost. Report planted-fault detection separately from real incident reduction. Zero failures in a small artificial test is neither universal safety nor measured harm prevented.

### Gaps

Owner agreement, data permissions, assessor capacity and operational logs are missing for all four proposals. No proposed date is an institutional commitment. New-cohort full graduation is unnecessary for an interim drill, but an artificial drill must never be relabelled a successful production safeguard.

## 3. How should this change comparisons, and what remains blocked?

### Takeaway

**Challenge India's and Japan's apparent lead with the same outcome standard, not by dismissing their better documentation.** Their advantage in the current model partly reflects recoverable upstream programme records; none of the compared routes yet demonstrates the exact proposed safeguard's complete causal chain. The proper next comparison is one matched operational test per route, with explicit differences in harm type and scope.

### Cited baseline and comparative implications

The existing [placement model](../../data/route-placement-model.json) assigns India **0.70 remedy** and Japan **0.70 remedy**, while Dubai, BPOM and Thailand have unknown remedies. The [prior synthesis](../../reports/Route%20coordinate%20evidence.md), particularly the India allocation and Japan procurement sections, nevertheless says India's lawful condition, consumed hours and governed payments are unresolved, and Japan's version-specific supplier remedy and observed enforcement remain missing. **Those are inherited findings, not newly re-audited India/Japan sources in this assignment.** Their stronger programme documentation is real; equating it with a demonstrated model-safety remedy is not justified by that report.

**Inference:** Thai contract discovery is an instructive counterexample to procurement-count optimism. An executed contract greatly improves institutional specificity, but its actual deliverable can still be training rather than a safety gate. Conversely, BPOM's narrow internal acceptance boundary might be easier to exercise than an upstream subsidy condition even though the programme is less prominent. These are conditional comparative hypotheses, not a new ranking. [Thai TOR §§4,7–9](https://www.etda.or.th/getattachment/efacff20-0fc7-4a24-84c9-68ffe84d3879/announce.pdf.aspx); [BPOM workflow](https://pusdatin.pom.go.id/berita/KATALISTIK-MORADA).

**Score implications, without edits:**

| Route | Better-supported update | What must remain unresolved |
|---|---|---|
| Dubai | Distinguish supplier qualification, application, service acceptance and live action authority | Exact independent assurer, hold and elapsed review time |
| Saudi CST | Concrete non-AI implementation; separate SDAIA alternative with named AI graduates | CST AI stop-rule; no borrowing SDAIA counts, clocks or powers |
| BPOM | Higher readiness for a narrowed MORADA integrity test | Production/DPIA authority and actual request denominator |
| Malaysia | More precise ADMP trigger and realistic standard-form buyer design | Enforced hold, selected controller and substantive correction |
| Thailand | Higher certainty of contracted operating capacity | Successor work order, participant deployment authority and accepted safety deliverables |
| Vietnam | Stronger functional applicability challenge | Signed local plan, qualifying decisional use, AI02 and actual restriction |

### Inferences

Use a common **evidence maturity ladder** for remedy: paper power; signed applicable obligation; implemented technical restriction; exercised failure/correction/retest; measured service outcome. Do not place one country's paper authority and another's exercised safeguard on the same rung. Separately score potential frontier reach and direct service-harm reduction: combining them conceals a policy value judgment.
