# Safeguard validation by Geneva

Independent research inputs for the next scenario and measurement pass, observed
31 October 2026. These are WIP investigations, not evidence of institutional
consent or completed pilots. Each JSON file owns its source-ID namespace.

- `geneva`: summit checkpoint, Swiss policy timetable and electricity-sector test.
- `apac`: Japan procurement, Singapore sandbox and Korean safety-case scenarios.
- `europe`: EuroHPC and UK consultation opportunities.
- `finance`: Canadian investment, Taiwanese compute awards and new-credit terms.
- `measurement`: measured exposure examples, agreement graphs and validation design.

The requested feasibility model tracks necessary institutions, handoffs,
signatures, decision rights, vetoes and source-supported political alignment.
General policy support is not acceptance of the proposed safeguard. Unknown
required-party denominators leave agreement ratios unidentified.

The requested impact model uses matched economic or supply-chain numerators and
denominators. Proxy coverage, enforceable control and safety effectiveness remain
separate quantities. The existing 1–5 map is not numerically rescaled by this pass.
