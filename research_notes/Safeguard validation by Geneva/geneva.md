# Geneva checkpoint and Swiss safeguard decision windows

Observation date: **31 October 2026**. Geneva is the deadline for selecting routes and collecting bounded trial evidence, not an external validator. Structured windows, candidate, measures and access ledger: `geneva.json`.

## 1. What is the checkpoint, and which formal channels precede it?

### Takeaway
The Swiss-hosted **Geneva AI Summit is 21–22 June 2027, Palexpo**. Distinguish it from ITU's AI for Good, **23–24 June**, whose exhibition begins on 21 June. Programme inputs and independent pre-events are participation channels, not safeguards approval. [S1](https://www.bakom.admin.ch/en/geneva-ai-summit-2027-international-summit-on-artificial-intelligence-in-geneva) [S2](https://genevaaisummit.swiss/)

### Cited Findings
- The official website announces a call for inputs in late/end-2026 and a side-event call in early 2027. Neither supplies an exact opening or closing day. Participant arrangements are also expected late-2026. [S2](https://genevaaisummit.swiss/)
- Road to Geneva registrations are open. Approved events receive a listing/logo; the organiser supplies neither funding nor organisational support. The FAQ describes registration closing both in “early June” and one week before the summit; do not manufacture a fixed June 14 deadline from these inconsistent descriptions. [S2](https://genevaaisummit.swiss/) [S3](https://genevaaisummit.swiss/pre-events)
- Strategic priorities combine prosperity with trustworthy, responsible and safe AI. The Federal Council seeks durable partnerships and initiatives; DETEC leads preparations with FDFA/EAER and the Delegate coordinates. Financial sponsorship does not determine agenda or political outcomes. This supports thematic fit, not consent to our route. [S4](https://www.eda.admin.ch/en/newnsb/McXrRqw1QIBiSfoeFwDlo)
- The inaugural UN Global Dialogue was a different Geneva process, 6–7 July 2026. Its UN page was retrievable by search/open, but direct fetch returned 403; no claim about a 2027 UN decision window follows. [S11](https://www.un.org/en/delegate-delegate-gva-delegate-nyc/inaugural-global-dialogue-ai-governance-convenes-geneva)

### Inferences
- **Proposed project gates:** select an owner/instrument by 31 January 2027, finish a bounded pilot by 31 May, and freeze findings by 14 June. These are our planning dates, not summit requirements or institutional commitments.
- Submit a method/early-result description when the call opens; an accepted pre-event or logo would establish participation only. Do not wait for an event invitation before conducting an authorised pilot.

### Gaps
- Exact input/side-event deadlines, selection rules and meeting-specific decision powers are not published in the retrieved pages. The detailed pre-event guidelines PDF was not extracted, so its additional restrictions remain unread.

## 2. What can change in Swiss policy before Geneva?

### Takeaway
Use the **28 October 2026 update: consultation opening is planned for spring 2027**, not a confirmed end-2026 public consultation. An older Federal Council decision asked for a consultation draft by end-2026; producing a draft and opening consultation are different milestones. The evidence does not establish whether the internal draft deadline was missed or formally replaced. [S5](https://www.bakom.admin.ch/de/newnsb/s2TQiUUjrA5D) [S6](https://www.bakom.admin.ch/en/artificial-intelligence) [S7](https://www.news.admin.ch/en/nsb?id=104110)

### Cited Findings
- FOJ/FDJP prepares binding implementation with OFCOM/DETEC and FDFA; the prospective rules cover transparency, data protection, nondiscrimination, supervision and risk assessment. Switzerland favours sector-specific amendments where possible rather than assuming a horizontal EU-style regime. [S6](https://www.bakom.admin.ch/en/artificial-intelligence)
- In parallel, OFCOM coordinates nonbinding guidelines, standards and voluntary commitments. The October release reports that the Swiss Federal Office of Energy has asked the electricity industry to develop guidelines and commitments addressing AI risks to electricity supply. This is a concrete policy opening; it is **not evidence of operator agreement or mandatory powers**. [S5](https://www.bakom.admin.ch/de/newnsb/s2TQiUUjrA5D)
- The October toolkit supplies modular voluntary-code language. The Federal Council's further instruction to establish internal knowledge exchange and develop the toolkit runs to end-March 2028, after Geneva. It is not a pre-summit implementation deadline. [S5](https://www.bakom.admin.ch/de/newnsb/s2TQiUUjrA5D)

### Inferences
- Spring consultation could incorporate evidence about operationally feasible risk assessment, incident escalation and human fallback. It cannot be treated as law in force, parliamentary approval, treaty ratification or a mandatory industry remedy by June.
- Current executive willingness is evidenced by work programmes and the electricity-sector request. Economic-freedom/innovation objectives and the voluntary design imply that a cheap, narrow pilot has a stronger institutional fit than a claim of government control over all AI infrastructure. This is an inference about fit, not measured political acceptance. [S5](https://www.bakom.admin.ch/de/newnsb/s2TQiUUjrA5D) [S6](https://www.bakom.admin.ch/en/artificial-intelligence)

### Gaps
- No retrieved enacted text, parliamentary calendar, consultation closing date, operator participation count, budget or signed commitment establishes adoption before Geneva. The 2026 sector report and toolkit PDFs were located but not extracted; do not attribute specific contractual clauses to them.

## 3. Which new route can be tested, and how should it be measured?

### Takeaway
**Proposal: electricity-sector AI fallback and incident drill under a voluntary Swiss industry commitment.** A willing electricity operator and its AI vendor agree a limited operational annex: named incident owner, tamper-evident records, authority to disable an AI advisory component, and a rehearsed human/conventional fallback. The public opening is SFOE's existing request to the industry; only consenting operators/vendor contracts can supply the proposed access and remedy. [S5](https://www.bakom.admin.ch/de/newnsb/s2TQiUUjrA5D)

### Cited Findings
- Swiss policy explicitly supports voluntary sector work while preserving innovation and economic freedom. The proposal exploits this available channel; it does not infer that SFOE has ordered a drill or that suppliers have accepted it. [S5](https://www.bakom.admin.ch/de/newnsb/s2TQiUUjrA5D) [S6](https://www.bakom.admin.ch/en/artificial-intelligence)
- World Bank data retrieved for **2024**, current US dollars: Switzerland GDP **969,919,786,394.884**, world GDP **111,669,432,109,121**, ratio approximately **0.008686** (0.8686%). The dataset reports an update of 8 October 2026. This is jurisdictional economic footprint, **not GDP subject to the proposed safeguard**. [S10](https://api.worldbank.org/v2/country/CHE;WLD/indicator/NY.GDP.MKTP.CD?date=2024&format=json)

### Inferences
- **Evidence-constrained skeptical ministry role-play — not testimony:** an SFOE official could object that electricity reliability is the mandate, vendor black boxes and staff time limit a pilot, and an AI shutdown could itself disrupt service. Response: start with one noncritical advisory workflow in a simulator, require an operator-owned conventional fallback, cap staff-hours, and test service continuity rather than claim a national off-switch. The mandate/voluntary opening is sourced; these objections, budget tolerance and vendor willingness are hypotheses to test.
- **Selection gate, proposed 31 January:** named operator and vendor consent; workflow boundary; access to logs; authorised disable/fallback control; realistic baseline; and a pre-agreed recovery-time objective. Stop if those are absent. Invitation, memorandum or role-play alone does not pass this gate.
- **Pilot, proposed February–May:** shadow/simulator only; an independent exercise designer preregisters 30 matched failure cases plus benign controls. Compare current procedure with the annex-assisted procedure on equivalent cases and counterbalance order. Measure critical-case containment without service violation, recovery time, evidence completeness, false shutdowns and staff-hours. Have an assessor score logs blind to arm where feasible; retain failed/aborted cases. No live-grid fault injection or dangerous attack details are needed.
- **Continuous axes:** X = intervention-arm safely contained cases / all preregistered intervention cases; report baseline rate, difference, paired uncertainty and elapsed time separately. Y = fraction of a declared electricity/AI dependency actually subject to the implemented safeguard. This is *demonstrated workflow effectiveness × scoped coverage information*, not a calibrated feasibility probability or catastrophic-risk-reduction estimate. If the existing chart retains those latter labels, this pilot does not justify filling its axes.
- **Proposed success gate:** at least 27/30 safely contained critical cases, at least six more successes than baseline on matched cases, no service-envelope breaches, and no material worsening on benign controls; recovery-time requirement is fixed by the operator before the trial. These are contestable design thresholds, not measurements or power calculations. Report all results and uncertainty even if the threshold is passed; 30 cases do not establish rare-event reliability.
- **Coverage, with explicit denominators:** (a) workflow safeguard coverage = in-scope AI execution hours on implemented controls / all AI execution hours in the defined operator estate; (b) Swiss electricity footprint = non-overlapping MWh delivered by participating operators / Swiss MWh delivered in the same period; (c) AI supply-chain coverage = MWh delivered to mapped AI/data-centre facilities with the dependency subject to verified fallback / total MWh serving that same defined facility population. All three are **unknown**, not zero. Metering boundaries, backup generation, load overlap and the actual connection from workflow to customer supply must be established. National GDP share must never substitute for (c).
- By Geneva one could validate the signed annex's scope, measured performance in the tested simulator distribution, evidence availability and actual participation. One could not validate frontier-model restraint, global adoption, elimination of grid risk, generalisation to unseen failure modes or reduced catastrophic risk. A signed code or completed paperwork proves neither operational effect nor risk reduction.

### Gaps
- No recruited operator, signed annex, baseline trial or reliable supply-chain denominator. Consequently there is no measured candidate X or Y point yet. National GDP contextual coverage is the only populated numeric coverage ratio, and is explicitly not route coverage.
- Next discriminating question: **will one electricity operator and its vendor provide a bounded workflow, logs and effective fallback authority by January, and what load actually depends on that workflow?** A negative answer should reject this pilot rather than turn a summit presentation into evidence.
