### Cited Findings
- The 26 October announcement says Lot 1 was extended so states could complete budget procedures and financial commitments; Lot 2 is unchanged. The same retained page still displays obsolete Lot 1 milestone metadata and a **25 November 2026, 23:59** last-question date. Do not infer that the latter was extended automatically. Evidence: `.local/finance-evidence/eurohpc-portal.txt`, lines 76–89, 149–157. [Contracting-authority portal](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/opportunities/tender-details/bc712395-692d-41ab-aa25-8e254ae86eb2-CN).
- The draft chain is **I.14.6 → II.18.1(p) → II.18.3**: notify security breaches without delay, keep authorities informed and implement mitigation; security/confidentiality violations supply a termination ground; formal notice, **15 days for observations**, then a final decision are required. A breach is not an automatic immediate shutdown. **II.11.2** prohibits amendments changing initial procurement conditions or causing unequal treatment. **I.15.2** requires end-user terms, offering a concrete place to investigate evidence flow-down. [Model FWC, pp.20–21,45,54–55](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/opportunities/tender-details/docs/bc712395-692d-41ab-aa25-8e254ae86eb2-CN/CfT%20AIGF%20-%20Annex%203%20-%20Model%20FWC%20v2_V2.pdf).
- Phase 1 targets four Lot 1 facilities of at least **25,000 H100-equivalent accelerators each**, and three Lot 2 facilities of at least **40,000 each**: **220,000 planned minimum equivalents**, not installed chips. EU Phase 1 procurement allocations total **€1bn**, matched at least by participating states; the two-phase EU ceiling is **€5bn**, with Phase 2 conditional on the next MFF. The portal's **€10bn estimated total** is not money spent. Payments start with operational readiness, following a technical audit; setup can take **18 months after FWC and SC1 signature**. [Specifications, §§3.d–f, pp.11–17](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/opportunities/tender-details/docs/bc712395-692d-41ab-aa25-8e254ae86eb2-CN/CfT%20AIGF%20-%20Tender%20Specifications%20v2_V1.pdf); [Annex 2, pp.5,8](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/opportunities/tender-details/docs/bc712395-692d-41ab-aa25-8e254ae86eb2-CN/CfT%20AIGF%20-%20Annex%202_V1.pdf).
- The commercial constraint is explicit: privately driven facilities, public anchor purchasing, operator-funded capital/operating expenditure, and some anchor-customer autonomy from sovereignty conditions. The FWC also anticipates restrictions on advanced US GPUs. These are constraints on a purchaser's reach, not proof that all tenant workloads are exempt from security reporting. [Specifications, pp.6–7,13–16,19](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/opportunities/tender-details/docs/bc712395-692d-41ab-aa25-8e254ae86eb2-CN/CfT%20AIGF%20-%20Tender%20Specifications%20v2_V1.pdf); [FWC I.14.3–7, p.20](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/opportunities/tender-details/docs/bc712395-692d-41ab-aa25-8e254ae86eb2-CN/CfT%20AIGF%20-%20Annex%203%20-%20Model%20FWC%20v2_V2.pdf).

### Inferences
- **Selection gate:** obtain an authoritative public clarification that a specified model-mediated data/security breach is covered; identify contractor, tenant evidence duties and reviewer; check any added requirement against procurement equality constraints. A broader model-harm reporting duty may require an actual amendment, not interpretation.
- **Simulated purchaser/operator stress test:** purchaser wants auditable notification; operator objects that opaque tenants and vendor contracts prevent comprehensive monitoring and that termination jeopardises financing. Proposed response: a narrow evidence schema, tenant terms and staged mitigation, preserving the contractual observations process. Whether any institution accepts this is unknown.
- **Before Geneva:** run a tabletop using synthetic breach evidence, including one anchor-tenant case. Blind reviewers classify scope, trace reporting to the authority, verify retained evidence, and exercise notice/observations. Measure correct-and-timely completed cases / all preregistered cases; separately report false positives and time-to-notification. This tests process and draft clarity, not operating gigafactory safety, prevented catastrophes or actual enforcement.
- **0–1 coverage:** planned minimum Lot 1 capacity / planned minimum total = 100,000/220,000 = **0.4545**; Lot 2 = **0.5455**. These compare the tender's own minimum capacities, not actual public compute access or world compute control. The defensible future exposure measure is covered contracted accelerator-hours / all contracted accelerator-hours for the same contracts, performance basis and period. Both values are currently missing. Keep the metric null rather than substitute GDP.

## Which additional late-2026 windows offer real input rights?

### Takeaway
Two UK calls are genuinely open: energy-system AI assurance closes **6 November 2026**; agentic-AI data-protection evidence closes **20 November 2026**. They permit evidence and proposed guidance, not unilateral new legal duties.

### Cited Findings
- DESNZ's call, issued **8 September 2026**, applies to England, Scotland and Wales. Questions 8–9 explicitly ask about system-level assurance and safe staging of autonomy. It feeds the first AI for Clean Energy Strategy; the document supplies no strategy publication deadline. It identifies adoption incentives, fragmented data, integration and vendor dependence as constraints, alongside affordability and security objectives. [Call and questions 8–9](https://www.gov.uk/government/calls-for-evidence/vision-for-an-ai-enabled-clean-energy-system/vision-for-an-ai-enabled-clean-energy-system-html).
- ICO's call opened **8 October 2026**, closes at the end of **20 November**, and covers security, accountability and other data-protection issues. Its roadmap targets **spring 2027** guidance without an exact date. Evidence also informs its statutory AI/automated-decision-making code; no date for that code is established here. [Call](https://ico.org.uk/AgenticAI); [Roadmap](https://ico.org.uk/about-the-ico/what-we-do/our-plans-for-new-and-updated-guidance/technology); [Scope and downstream use](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/artificial-intelligence/the-ico-s-call-for-evidence-on-agentic-ai/).
- EU high-risk implementation is a separate timing issue. The Commission now states **2 December 2027**, and describes the political impetus as simplification and competitiveness. An application date does not establish a still-open standards consultation or power to add a new safeguard. No such EU drafting window was substantiated in this bounded pass. [Commission May 7 announcement](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/eu-agrees-simplify-ai-rules-boost-innovation-and-ban-nudification-apps-protect-citizens); [Current policy overview](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai).

### Inferences
- **Energy candidate:** propose a staged-autonomy assurance template requiring a named override owner, tested fallback and change-triggered retesting. Pilot one historical/replayed operator workload with correlated vendor outages and compare existing versus proposed safeguards. Success: safe fallback within a predeclared operational envelope / all preregistered fault episodes, with operator workload and cost measured separately. A simulator tests the assumed system and process; it does not establish grid-wide reliability improvement. Stop if no operator/dataset or lawful deployment instrument is identified.
- **ICO candidate:** propose auditable controller/processor allocation, bounded tool permissions and incident evidence across an agentic supply chain. Run synthetic personal-data workflows through baseline and proposed controls; count blocked unauthorised actions / all preregistered unauthorised attempts, plus false refusals / authorised tasks. Have independent reviewers reconstruct responsibility from logs. This tests technical controls and accountability usability, not ICO approval or general catastrophic-risk reduction.
- **Simulated objections:** the energy operator resists automation that adds delay or conflicts with dispatch duties; the agentic deployer resists logs exposing personal data or vendor IP. Responses to test: shadow-mode fallback with operator-set timing, and minimal/redacted logs with explicit access permissions. These are design hypotheses, not stakeholder testimony.

### Gaps
- Neither call grants budget, access to operators or regulator commitment to adopt a proposal. No affected MW, agent deployments, procurement amounts or matching economic denominator were found. Coverage stays null. A spring guidance target may slip; no exact completion date should be invented.

## What can be selected and measured by Geneva?

### Takeaway
Select on documented authority and attainable evidence, then report adoption, contractual coverage and test performance separately. Do not manufacture a universal feasibility or safety probability from these measurements.

### Cited Findings
- EuroHPC's dated call and draft obligations provide a specific contract pathway; UK consultations provide actual input pathways. Their legal effects and populations differ. [EuroHPC](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/opportunities/tender-details/bc712395-692d-41ab-aa25-8e254ae86eb2-CN); [DESNZ](https://www.gov.uk/government/calls-for-evidence/vision-for-an-ai-enabled-clean-energy-system); [ICO](https://ico.org.uk/AgenticAI).

### Inferences
- Proposed internal gates: by **15 December 2026**, locate a responsible owner and evidence-access route; by **28 February 2027**, fix a lawful instrument, baseline, denominator and test protocol; by **31 May 2027**, finish independent replay and publish failures; by **21 June 2027**, label each route selected, rejected or unresolved. These are project proposals, not official windows.
- Display 0–1 **covered exposure**, **observed protocol completion**, and **test performance**, each with sample size and uncertainty. A selected subset must remain a subset of the stated denominator. Never combine planned capacity with installed capacity, EU money with total public/private money, or nominal chips with performance equivalents. GDP is economic context only, not a control-right denominator.
- Prefer EuroHPC for an instrument-clarity demonstration; energy for system-level fallback testing; ICO for a feasible agent-workflow prototype and a spring-2027 guidance comparison. None yet merits an empirical estimate of catastrophic-risk reduction.
