### Inferences
Proposed lender argument: justify evidence conditions through credit protection, cost and borrower acceptance. Proposed borrower argument: existing commitments or competing finance may be preferable. Neither represents actual party consent. No arranger-nationality inference is permitted.

**Proposed tests, not external deadlines:** January 31, 2027 selection gate; February 1–May 31 measurement period. Require identifiable signatories, lawful discretion, accepted terms and a fixed denominator before selecting. For each scenario report separately:

1. **Rights:** covenanted new money / total same-transaction new money; or covered allocated GPU-hours / all same-cohort allocated GPU-hours. Tokens are a separate fraction.
2. **Implementation:** independently verified timely deliveries / all deliveries actually due.
3. **Test efficacy:** independently corrected-and-retested failures / predeclared baseline failures, with a comparison cohort where feasible, counts and uncertainty.

All are presently **null**, not zero or a model-assigned score. Empty denominators mean unmeasured. Tabletop reporting tests workflow only. A signed appendix cannot demonstrate reduced catastrophic risk; a successful retest concerns only the specified test set. Record withdrawals, migration, reporting burden and failed remedies rather than excluding them from the denominator.

### Gaps
Anthropic's actual holders, amounts, new-credit schedule and priced alternatives; executed coverage in all three routes; actual safety observations and a defensible counterfactual. Stop if discretion or consent is absent; do not call no incidents a safety success. No additional candidate was added merely to increase sample size.
