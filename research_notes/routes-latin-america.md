## Measurement and institutional scope

`coverage:null` for all four. None of the retrieved material pairs the proposed safeguard's covered deployments with all eligible deployments. Brazil's **20 planned activities / half in H1 2027** is programme scheduling context, not a model count, pilot allocation, achieved inspection count or safety coverage fraction [br-anpd-map-30]. H1 ends on 30 June, after Geneva. Do not turn it into an assumed 10/20 safeguard fraction. No GDP measure is used.

Proposed denominator to collect: all in-scope AI deployments in a selected body's or inspection cohort's fixed observation period. Numerator: those with the agreed test dossier and completed remediation/retest decision. Also retain number of tests, unresolved failures and the original failure severity; passing a dossier-completeness metric is not evidence of reduced catastrophic risk.

Each agreement scope lists a regulator and an unidentified regulated/deploying entity. These are role categories, not a verified count of independently required institutions. Therefore all handoff lower bounds remain null, not zero or a guessed one. Identify the actual entities, vendor/processor dependencies and powers first; for a verified connected path of N independent institutions the lower bound must be at least N−1. Internal delegations, council members, transparency committees and receiving units must not inflate N.

All consent and alignment fields are unknown. A statutory duty is not agreement to a new benchmark. Mandatory lawful supervision does not give controllers a consent veto; cooperation with experimental tests beyond an established duty is a separate question. Mexico's regulator recommendations are explicitly nonbinding, so the deploying body's adoption of the release condition matters [mx-public-data-law]. Legislative bodies affecting Chile's commencement are recorded as an external dependency, not invented pilot signatories.

2. **Mexico:** locate a named federal-executive deployment's assessment and the current receiving-unit/guidance instrument. Check arts.69/73 applicability before recruiting. 3. **Chile:** first retrieve final proceedings for **bill 18.623-07**, both houses' outcomes and any promulgated amendment. Then check agency readiness and issued art.15 ter guidance. The bill threatens the entire legal-before-Geneva premise. Proposed fallback: a controller's voluntary readiness study with the same failure/remediation/retest record, labelled voluntary throughout. No implied agency endorsement.
4. **Colombia:** retrieve a current SIC case/work-plan slot and administrator identity. Then obtain the high-risk study and security controls under Circular 002. Proposed pilot: establish whether a preidentified privacy failure survives the stated controls and whether a repair remains effective on a held-out test. SIC's own contract policy is an alternative lead only after retrieving the actual contract, authority and future award date.

Shared proposed timeline: select entity and freeze scope in late 2026/early 2027; establish baseline by March; document mitigation by May; retest and deliver a limitations statement by 15 June. These are planning targets. No source promises them. Retain nonparticipation, exemptions and failed retests rather than selecting only successful cases. Report adoption, implementation and changed behaviour separately; no causal claim about advanced-AI risk reduction follows from one pilot.
