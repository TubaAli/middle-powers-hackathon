## 3. Who can actually exercise financial leverage, and where are the middle-power openings?

The best-supported levers lie with **contract signatories, credit committees, asset owners and conditional capital providers**. Nationality or a portfolio holding is not itself a government decision right. Two especially concrete middle-power links are Japan's SoftBank/MUFG financing relationships and the UAE's MGX participation in an acquired infrastructure owner. ### Inferences

- **Proposal, not an existing covenant:** approach a new-project credit committee or infrastructure equity sponsor with measurable incident reporting, independent pre-deployment evaluation, protected model weights and continuity planning as underwriting conditions. Make them conditions of *new* capital or negotiated amendments, with reporting and remedy language, rather than presuming the power to rewrite executed agreements. The contract-backed facilities identify a possible institutional entry point, not consent. [CoreWeave financing](https://investors.coreweave.com/news/news-details/2026/CoreWeave-Closes-Landmark-8-5-Billion-Financing-Facility-Achieving-First-Investment-Grade-Rated-GPU-backed-Financing/default.aspx)
- **Coalition incentive test:** lenders and asset owners may support loss-prevention/security provisions but resist rules that impair utilization or collateral cash flows. Labs can seek alternative suppliers; Microsoft explicitly permits multi-cloud distribution and Anthropic uses multiple cloud platforms. Coalition effectiveness therefore depends on covering credible substitutes, not simply summing investment shares. [Microsoft amendment](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/04/27/the-next-phase-of-the-microsoft-openai-partnership/); [Anthropic partnerships](https://www.anthropic.com/news/microsoft-nvidia-anthropic-announce-strategic-partnerships)
- **Next discriminating question:** Which currently uncommitted tranche or refinancing has a decision-maker willing and legally able to add a measurable safety covenant, and what alternatives could the borrower obtain? Request that agreement's draw conditions, amendment-vote thresholds, reporting undertakings and remedies before assigning a “credible leverage” score.
