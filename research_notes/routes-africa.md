### South Africa — narrow data-linkage gate

**Pilot proposal:** identify a responsible party proposing AI-assisted identifier linkage that actually meets both changed-purpose and linkage-with-another-responsible-party conditions. Check codes/exemptions before interpreting a standstill. Ask for an annex with match errors, provenance, access tests, mitigation and correction evidence. Do not treat any use of identifiers or any AI processing as sufficient.

**Decision architecture:** applicant → Information Regulator. The information officer is internal. Other data holders, supplier access and sector permissions may add parties. The one-interface lower bound is structural, not a list of secured agreements. The regulator's legal decision and the applicant's voluntary participation are different decisions.

**Validation proposal:** select by December 2026; obtain an actual application timestamp, reviewed evidence and a redacted decision; retest failures before Geneva. Failure condition: no qualifying application or no obtainable decision/evidence. Merely filing a form is not a working safeguard.
