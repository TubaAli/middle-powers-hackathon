## Testable pilots and decisive gaps

These are proposed research steps, not contacts made or institutional commitments. Target sequencing is optional project design: identify a case by January 2027, agree evidence and threshold by March, run and retest in April–May, inspect the actual decision by 20 June. No official source promises these dates.

### Indonesia — `indonesia-bpom-dpia-gate`

**Candidate experiment:** choose one BPOM AI-assisted workflow handling personal data. Start with a synthetic dataset and one preregistered critical leakage/automated-decision failure. Ask the eventual authorised owner to demonstrate that this failure causes an internal deployment hold; fix and retest it. Obtain a signed assessment and production-access record. This is a proposal, not evidence that BPOM currently operates such a system.

**Authority gap:** identify the system owner, production signatory and any indispensable processor before treating the route as feasible. Resolve PP 33/2026 and current supervisory establishment before relying on an external sanction. BPOM's implementation support and July deputy-minister remarks are dated administrative signals, not proof of presidential or current regulator endorsement of this pilot.

**Missing country findings:** no verified public-AI tender, public-compute award condition or named qualifying rollout with a 2026/27 close date. The gap does not imply such opportunities do not exist. The present route is supported by the statutory DPIA instrument and a concrete public body's compliance work.

### Malaysia — `malaysia-commercial-ai-dpia`

**Candidate experiment:** an outsourced commercial AI service handling customer data. Agree a synthetic, held-out leakage and harmful-decision test; place failure and mitigation evidence in the assessment dossier. Inspect whether a failed threshold blocks production and whether the processor supplies the evidence promised in its contract. A paper assessment without a changed deployment decision fails our mechanism test.

**Authority gap:** management's ability to accept residual risk is precisely why a new written hold is needed. Regulator pre-approval is not presumed. Choose the controller and processor before counting approvals. Do not place federal/state deployments in Act 709 coverage merely because the instrument is issued by government. No national leader's position on this exact hold has been established.

**Missing country findings:** no matched affected-resource pool and no verified 2027 tender, grant intake or final AI-framework milestone. The October consultation is already closed. It supplies policy context, not an open submission opportunity.

### Thailand — `thailand-aigpc-sandbox-gate`

**Candidate experiment:** a domestic public-service chatbot owner and its separate developer join an ETDA-facilitated case. Agree a held-out Thai-language test, record remediation and make the wider-rollout decision inspectable. Evaluate test reproducibility and actual use of the hold separately. A workshop or test report alone does not satisfy the decision test.

**Authority gap:** obtain admission terms, a named owner, a developer evidence agreement and dates before claiming a pilot can finish before Geneva. The programme director's 5 October statement supports practical risk assessment but does not confer a legal veto. ETDA/AIGPC is one institution. External experts count only if a separately necessary institutional approval is demonstrated.

**Missing country findings:** no binding public procurement clause or signed sandbox admission condition retrieved. Retain Low confidence because the route presently rests on an announced operational programme, not an executed gate. It is a weaker instrument than the Vietnam provision, and should be displayed that way.

### Vietnam — `vietnam-public-ai-impact-gate`

**Candidate experiment:** identify one qualifying public-service system and its AI02 assessment; agree the critical-failure threshold with the deploying agency. Run a synthetic harmful-decision scenario, show human intervention, and inspect the approval decision after remediation. Seek a publishable redacted dossier rather than assume unrestricted access.

**Authority gap:** confirm the actual agency head's delegation and the selected system's scope; identify any sector regulator or supplier dependency. MOST supervision does not automatically add a second launch signature. Government enactment is evidence of the assessment policy, not a personal commitment by the current minister to our chosen threshold.

**Missing country findings:** no identified pre-June deployment, completed assessment or observed refused approval. Article 20 is a real instrument but its practical leverage remains to be demonstrated.

## Measurement plan

For each selected pilot, define an eligible pool **before** observing results: all production-bound versions in that named pilot and time interval. Numerator: eligible versions for which the agreed assessment, evidence access and exercised decision rule are all documented. Denominator: every eligible version, including cancelled or failed ones. This would measure pilot-control coverage, not national AI coverage or safety impact. Report test failure/mitigation rates separately with a fixed test-set denominator; never relabel them as resource coverage.

Keep four endpoints distinct: (1) terms adopted, (2) evaluation executed, (3) deployment changed because of the evaluation, (4) real-world harm reduction. The first three may be testable before Geneva after access is secured; the fourth is not established by these proposed pilots. The next discriminating question is which named owner will allow observation of an actual failed-test deployment decision.
