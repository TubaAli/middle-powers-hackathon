What the numbers mean

Evidence-informed model · all 32 routes

These continuous 0–100 indices are weighted editorial assessments based on the retrieved 31 October 2026 evidence. They are not measured impact, probabilities, or a new empirical dataset. Comparisons across different safety outcomes depend on judgment.

Feasibility: 40% authority/operational readiness + 30% timing before Geneva + 30% manageable coordination. Potential safety impact if adopted: 40% possible reach + 30% safety relevance + 30% ability to enforce a remedy.

Each factor uses a 0–1 rubric. Missing factors receive a neutral 0.5 prior. There is no automatic bonus for coalition size or source count. The selected route shows its rationale, exact factor scores, imputations and linked evidence.

The shaded sensitivity box varies each assessed factor by ±0.15 and each imputed factor by ±0.30, with fixed weights and scores clipped to 0–1. It is not a statistical confidence interval and does not capture all model uncertainty. Small deterministic noise and collision offsets move only the drawn circles; the cross and displayed numbers retain the original scores.

Potential coverage

Covered allocation ÷ total allocation in the selected pool, if the proposed terms are agreed. Different pools are shown separately. This is reach, not harm prevented.

Agreement index

Support among required parties ÷ (1 + minimum unresolved handoffs). Missing consent gives a range. Any required party’s confirmed objection blocks that path. An incomplete path may need more parties.

This is an explicit comparison model, not a calibrated forecast. General policy support is recorded separately from agreement to the safeguard.
