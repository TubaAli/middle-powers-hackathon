What the numbers mean

Potential coverage

Covered allocation ÷ total allocation in the selected pool, if the proposed terms are agreed. Different pools are shown separately. This is reach, not harm prevented.

Agreement index

Support among required parties ÷ (1 + minimum unresolved handoffs). Missing consent gives a range. Any required party’s confirmed objection blocks that path. An incomplete path may need more parties.

This is an explicit comparison model, not a calibrated forecast. General policy support is recorded separately from agreement to the safeguard.
