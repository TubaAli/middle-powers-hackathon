Implementation feasibility →

1: major unresolved authority or consent barriers. 3: plausible instrument, material agreements still needed. 5: a narrow action using established machinery with few unresolved dependencies. Ratings concern the full described route, not just writing a proposal.

Potential safety impact ↑

1: indirect or narrowly scoped benefit. 3: a concrete failure mode addressed across several uses. 5: potentially broad prevention of severe advanced-AI harm. All ratings are conditional judgments. None is a measured effect size.

Confidence ≠ feasibility

Confidence concerns evidence for the mechanism. A signed instrument can be well documented but difficult to change. A draft is not an active safeguard. Ranges show reasonable editorial disagreement, not statistical confidence intervals.

Colour describes dependencies

National routes may still require suppliers’ agreement. Middle-power routes require coordination across jurisdictions. Broader routes need additional actors, such as EU institutions or a US borrower. Colours never automatically improve ratings.

What would make this live?

Monitor primary sources, discover underexplored routes, retrieve contradictory evidence, and propose traceable revisions. This version loads a dated, curated snapshot; it does not continuously scan literature.

AI-assisted, human-accountable

Research and implementation used OpenAI, Anthropic and GLM tools. Source claims, proposals and editorial assessments are separated. Inspect the sources and unresolved questions before relying on a route.
