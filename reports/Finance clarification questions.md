# Finance leverage: next evidence to request

**Draft for team use, 31 October 2026. Not sent.** These questions identify missing evidence; they do not assert an institution's consent or authority.

## Priority 1: EuroHPC contracting authority

**Purpose:** distinguish existing security obligations from a new model-safety requirement.

> Under Articles I.14.6–I.14.7 of the model Framework Contract, what duties apply when an incident originates in a model or service operated by an anchor customer but affects the security or integrity of the AIGF? Please distinguish the contractor's reporting and mitigation duties from duties owed directly by the customer.

> Can a Specific Contract request under Article 11.5 specify incident-notification formats, retained evidence and a tested tenant-isolation procedure within the published requirements? Which elements require agreement or a contract amendment under Article II.11, and which would alter the original procurement conditions?

> Please identify the applicable user terms or written flow-down implementing Articles I.14.3(c) and I.15.2, and explain whether any published requirement provides independent evaluator access to customer models. We have not identified an explicit pre-release model-evaluation right.

**Ask for:** relevant published Q&A, completed model user terms, contractual interpretation and version identifiers. **Success criterion:** an exact duty, obligor, beneficiary, trigger, verification route and remedy, with procurement compatibility clarified. A statement that a new tender or agreement is needed is also a useful result.

## Priority 2: Canadian investment authority

> Which legal entity makes the announced Cohere equity investment, and has its subscription been executed? For later stages, are funds unconditionally committed, subject to specified conditions precedent, subject to certifications/milestones, or subject to further investment decisions?

> What deliverables implement the commitment to continue working with CAISI, and what progress is reported against that commitment? Who receives and verifies the evidence, and what remedies or funding consequences, if any, are agreed?

**Ask for:** publishable transaction summary, exact investing vehicle, stage conditions, safety-cooperation arrangement, and reporting template. Redacted terms or an official explanation can answer the question without assuming that confidential agreements will be disclosed. **Success criterion:** evidence of actual remaining discretion and a defined safety deliverable. Do not presume CDEV is the holder.

## Priority 3: Anthropic financing evidence

> Can a public or publishable source identify actual lenders and commitments under the September 2026 facility, and whether a new incremental facility, extension or refinancing is currently being sought?

> For that specific prospective transaction, which parties must agree to an additional reporting/evaluation covenant, and would it survive repayment, refinancing, assignment or amendment? Who would verify compliance and enforce the remedy?

**Ask for:** an available commitment schedule or lender disclosure, relevant new-financing document, consent thresholds and covenant terms. **Success criterion:** a willing decision-maker with a genuinely open financing decision. A bank's arranger title or headquarters is insufficient. Existing compliant draws are not a new negotiation opportunity merely because payment has not yet occurred.

## Proposed pilot deliverable

Create one evidence card with a defined incident/security obligation first. Show separately any proposed extension to model evaluation or developer reporting. Test whether the obligation reaches the relevant model developer and remains enforceable after the initial financing decision. Do not contact parties or represent the draft as agreed without team authorisation.
