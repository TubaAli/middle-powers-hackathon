# AI trends to Geneva: evidence and fictional effects

Research cutoff: 1 November 2026. This synthesis links to the detailed retrieval ledgers; it does not add new retrieval.

## What shipped

The Orbit Accord release `ab4e84a` adds research links, an AI outlook and fictional stress-test epilogues through `game/orbit-research.js`. Its global outlook extends Epoch's historical 5× annual training-compute trend, with 4–6× alternative rates, across the campaign to Geneva. These are extrapolations, not predictive confidence intervals or catastrophe probabilities. The separate coalition-rollout curve reduces the growth exponent by up to 35% with safety-goal progress. That coefficient is invented. Historical fit intervals neither validate nor constrain policy-effect sizes.

## What remains unknown

The research does not estimate causal policy-effect coefficients, catastrophe probabilities, autonomous-write prevalence or a universal measure of oversight quality. Source publication dates, event dates and retrieval dates are recorded separately in the linked notes. Earlier abstract-only synthetic-data claims must not override the source check.

The next discriminating question is whether labs publish comparable per-release monitorability and incident-transparency results, with independent access sufficient to check them, before Geneva. These are proposed observables, not additional shipped game mechanics.
