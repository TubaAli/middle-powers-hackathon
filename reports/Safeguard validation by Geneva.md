# Safeguards to test before Geneva

**Decision brief · evidence observed 31 October 2026 · Geneva AI Summit: 21–22 June 2027** ([official dates][summit])

**Pursue five tests.** These are research choices. We have not verified institutional agreement to them.

| Where | What to test |
|---|---|
| EuroHPC | Can incident reports reach the right authority and lead to action? |
| Japan | Does safety evidence still apply when a deployed model changes? |
| Singapore | Can a buyer stop an agent release that fails testing? |
| Switzerland | Can an electricity operator safely fall back from AI? |
| UK | Do agent permissions and logs prevent misuse and show who is responsible? |

**First deadlines, all in 2026:**
- **6 November:** UK energy evidence. ([Call][energy])
- **20 November:** UK agentic-AI evidence. ([ICO][ico])
- **3 December:** EuroHPC Lot 2 bids. ([Announcement][portal])

**What we can measure by June:** whether agreed controls work in specific tests, and how much of a defined programme they cover. That would not establish reduced catastrophic risk.

**Check before starting:** who can approve the change, what the current rules already require, and which users or systems the test covers. No route yet has enough evidence to calculate its safeguard coverage or full approval fraction.

## Next decisions

Switzerland's **end-2026 internal draft target** and **spring-2027 public consultation opening** are separate steps; the latter comes from the latest, 28 October update. **Japan already asks vendors for safety evidence.** Its original call requires comparative benchmarks, explanations of hallucination/bias/harmful-content controls, and partial-publication consent (conditions, second and seventh bullets). The FY2027 plan requests tool-use, safety and supply-term information (§2, items ④, ⑥, ⑨, ⑩). The test should add something specific: **evidence tied to the deployed version, tests of agents that retain state, and duties to act on incidents and changes**. Another request for safety explanations would repeat the baseline. ([Japan original][jp-old]; [plan][jp-plan])

**Taiwan already has reporting and enforcement terms.** ADI's rules specify allocations, performance reports, logs and particular suspension/termination provisions (physical pp.3, 8–9, 13–14). The addition would be **model-specific safety evidence and independently checked fixes**, agreed for future awards. The next review does not itself allow new terms for existing applications. ## What we can measure

Measure **how much a working safeguard covers** and **how many required approvals are documented**. Neither tells us how much risk falls or the probability of delivery. Both need complete counts before they can replace the existing 1–5 editorial ratings. Dividing those ratings by five would not create measurements. The formulas and evidence requirements follow. ([Measurement design][measurement]; [public data][data])

**Reach:** `C = Σ(wᵢ × coveredᵢ) / Σwᵢ`, for a positive, frozen eligible denominator. Count operational coverage only where the obligation is effective, evidence collection works and a usable remedy applies; record signed-only coverage separately. Weights must be observed quantities: covered versus all eligible procurement payments, delivered accelerator-hours of comparable hardware, contracts, or stage-specific output. Economic activity coverage needs **attributable GVA** and a legal-to-firm/activity crosswalk; GDP additionally needs consistent product-tax allocation. National GDP share supplies context, not legal reach. Do not mix tokens with GPU-hours, allocated with delivered capacity, or planned minimums with installed stock. Union overlapping subjects and final units: adding EU and member-state GDP or serial supply-chain percentages double-counts.

**Readiness:** freeze a complete, legally sufficient set of `N` required approval acts. With `S` evidenced valid exact-scope approvals and `U` genuinely unresolved states, show **documented `S/N` and evidence bounds `[S/N, (S+U)/N]`**. Confirmed pending or opposed acts are not unknown. An incomplete denominator means **null**, not an estimated midpoint. Display independent institutions, required signatures and cross-owner handoffs separately; evaluate compulsory **AND**, alternative **OR** and actual legal voting thresholds. One indispensable blocker overrides an otherwise high fraction. Generic leader support has no numerical encoding. Finite approval counts yield discrete ratios on a 0–1 axis; continuous-looking interpolation would invent evidence. Completion dates additionally require durations, resource constraints and accepted handoffs.

Historical LUMI allocation shares are not transferred to the new gigafactory route. A multi-year equity ceiling divided by annual GDP, or gross procurement expenditure divided by GDP, is not a bounded subset-coverage measure and is excluded. **Delivery probability remains null:** no comparable-route outcome cohort or calibrated model was retrieved. Such a forecast would require prespecified comparable attempts, failures, withdrawals, censoring and held-out-time calibration—not multiplied guesses about leaders. ([Measurement limitations][measurement])

## Tests before June

Report each step separately: **research choice → institutional agreement → adoption → working evidence and remedies → changed behaviour → reduced harm**. First record current practice, including Japan and Taiwan's existing duties. Define the test population, tasks, failures and treatment of missing data before testing. Compare current and proposed procedures on matched tasks, with independent review of cases not used to develop the controls. Keep failed and aborted tests, costs, false refusals, delays and migration in the results. Success rates lie between 0 and 1; improvement can be negative. Testing in another lab can show whether results transfer, but it does not create international recognition. ([Protocol and audit design][measurement]; [pipeline differences][sgkr])

For Swiss replay, **27 safe containments out of 30 critical cases**, at least six more than the matched baseline, and no service-envelope violation are **proposed thresholds**, not results or a justified statistical power calculation. The operator must set recovery criteria. For agent gates, require an actual held or remediated release/permission decision as well as test performance. For EuroHPC, exercise notice and the contractual **15-day observations procedure** rather than imagining immediate automatic shutdown; setup can take **18 months after signature**, so operational-facility outcomes cannot be promised by June. For award and finance routes, no reports due means compliance is unmeasured, not perfect. ([Swiss proposal][geneva-notes]; [contract][fwc]; [specifications][spec])

Proposed project checkpoints are **15 December** for fixed candidate definitions, **31 January** for owner/rights/baseline selection, **31 March** for instrumentation, **31 May** for completed pilots, **7 June** for data lock and **14 June** for independent review. They are not official windows; earlier consultation and procurement deadlines take precedence, and Japan's April deployment plan requires a later operational test. July–December 2027 monitoring and retesting remain post-summit follow-through. The Commission-reported **2 December 2027** timing for specified high-risk AI rules and Switzerland's **end-March 2028** toolkit follow-up likewise do not supply pre-Geneva implementation evidence. ([Commission announcement—not consolidated law][eu-timing]; [Swiss update][swiss])

## Unanswered questions

**Will one authorised owner agree to a test, provide the records and act on a failure?** We also need to know which users or systems the safeguard covers and what it adds to current practice. Record refusals and failed tests as findings. Before extending a test across countries, check that its results transfer and that each purchaser accepts them.

The route table identifies the missing approvals and documents. There are also source limits: Anthropic's credit evidence is retained tool-extracted text, not independently recovered raw SEC content, and its prospectus liquidity analysis is unavailable. Swiss toolkit, sector-report and pre-event-guideline PDFs remain unread. This report uses the supplied investigations; no institution has been contacted or recruited and no proposed experiment has been run.
